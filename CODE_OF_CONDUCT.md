# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors
- Being respectful of differing quantitative methodologies, risk models, and viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback
- Prioritizing reproducible benchmarks, honest reporting of backtest assumptions, and clear risk disclosures

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
- Publishing fabricated live-trading profit claims or concealing lookahead bias in benchmarks
- Publishing others' private information or API credentials without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository maintainers via GitHub Issues or Security Advisories. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
